# Vibium — renewal handoff

**App:** Vibium browser automation CLI.

**Persona:** A customer-success coordinator preparing a renewal handoff.

**Mission:** On a deterministic local renewal page, assign the account to Ana, select the expansion tier, enable the notification, and confirm the saved summary.

**Journey:** Start an isolated headless browser session, open the local page, inspect its accessible controls, enter the owner one character at a time, choose the tier, enable notifications, save, and read the confirmation.

**Value demonstrated:** Vibium turns a small but real operational handoff into inspectable browser actions with semantic selectors and an explicit outcome.
