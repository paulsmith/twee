# Herdr — incident triage desk

**App:** Herdr terminal agent multiplexer.

**Persona:** An engineering lead setting up a focused incident-triage desk.

**Mission:** Set up a focused incident desk and keep a concrete incident handoff beside its diagnostic checklist.

**Journey:** Complete Herdr's deterministic first-run setup, select the Tokyo Night theme, split the workspace to the right, type a checkout-latency handoff into the incident pane, type the p95/error-rate check into the diagnostic pane, then move focus across the workspace and sidebar.

**Value demonstrated:** Herdr’s mouse-native workflow turns an active incident into a readable two-pane desk where the handoff and its next diagnostic are visible together.
