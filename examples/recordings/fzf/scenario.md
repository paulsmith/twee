# fzf runbook selection

- **App:** fzf
- **Persona:** Luis, a release engineer responding to a failed deployment alert.
- **Mission:** Find the correct rollback runbook from a compact operational catalog and state the next action.
- **Journey:** Luis opens the runbook picker, narrows it to the deployment entry, confirms it, and echoes the selected runbook and immediate response.
- **Value demonstrated:** fzf makes a focused operational lookup quick while retaining a normal shell-based follow-through.
